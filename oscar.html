About

Lando Norris is a British Formula One driver currently racing for McLaren. Born on 13 November 1999 in Bristol, England, Norris is widely regarded as one of the most talented drivers of his generation. His journey to Formula One began at a very young age, progressing through karting and junior racing categories with remarkable success.

After winning multiple junior championships, including the FIA Formula 2 Championship runner-up position in 2018, Norris earned a seat with McLaren and made his Formula One debut in 2019. From his rookie season onward, he impressed fans and experts alike with his racecraft, consistency, and ability to perform under pressure against far more experienced drivers.
